# 5 — LATAM fintech treasury

**Company:** Ballast (go-to-market for 1–3)

## Problem

Brazilian and Argentine fintechs hold idle treasury in dollars or local cash. Local government
debt and yield-bearing dollar tokens exist on Stellar, but there is no liquidity or collateral
use for them.

## Evidence

- Stellar overtook Ethereum in tokenized non-US government debt: about $490M (RWA.xyz via SDF,
  2026-08-20).
- Etherfuse TESOURO (Brazil) is live but thin: about $400k supply and 94 funded trustlines.
  CETES is about $4.1M.
- Figure YLDS launched on Stellar on 2026-05-05, aimed at LATAM fintechs and neobanks.

## Why not alone

The assets are too thin to support a company. The customers, though, are the anchors Ballast
already sells to, and they need exactly ideas 1 and 3: yield on treasury, usable as prefunding
collateral, exitable instantly. This is Ballast's first market, not a separate business.

## Kill criteria

- Fintechs' regulators forbid holding tokenized sovereign debt as treasury.
- Etherfuse or Figure bundle collateral and exit themselves.
